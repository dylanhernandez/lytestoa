About this game

Rubix is an arcade-style Rubik’s cube solver with a cyberpunk / sci-fi theme. Built from scratch with pure HTML5 Canvas and vanilla JavaScript, it uses no external libraries and no assets.

The cube starts shuffled. Rotate its slices with the on-screen buttons — three clockwise turns equal one counter-clockwise turn — and use the sliders to spin the view a full 360°. The timer starts on your first move: solve all six faces to finish, then chase your best time. Records persist in your browser.

How to play

  • Tap anywhere or press any key to start.
  • Rotate slices with the L / M / R (vertical) and U / M / D (horizontal) buttons.
  • Inspect the cube with the view sliders — full 360° rotation.
  • RESET VIEW snaps back to the front face; SHUFFLE starts a new scramble; QUIT abandons the run without a score.
